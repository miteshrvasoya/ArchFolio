The Process

Every space is born out of its context: the climate, the terrain and the way a family gathers from morning tea to dusk. We do not impose a fixed formula; each site — in Surat, Ahmedabad or the Indian Himalayas — guides its own architectural response.

With a core palette of board-formed concrete, handcrafted brick, natural stone and reclaimed timber, we test how light grazes every texture, from a 1:500 site plan down to a custom door handle.

  1. 01

    Formwork

    Shuttering patterns and tie-rod rhythms drawn before a single column is poured.

  2. 02

    Joinery

    Hand-turned timber profiles and tactile brass hardware crafted with local artisans.

  3. 03

    Microclimate

    Solar angles and breeze tested through physical brick jaali mock-ups.

  4. 04

    Sun & Shadow

    Choreographing how winter sunlight and foliage shadows move across floors through the day.

Principal & Founder Architect

Ar. Ayush Vasoya

I am an architect based in Surat, dedicated to crafting grounded, climate-responsive spaces that balance honest materiality with contemporary living. My approach was shaped by hands-on practice in the Indian Himalayas, where designing for sensitive mountain terrain and extreme seasons taught me the value of passive solar planning, contextual resilience and vernacular wisdom.

Today I bring that environmental sensitivity back to Gujarat — private villas, weekend retreats and urban residences built from exposed concrete, natural stone and brick, with thoughtful indoor-outdoor connections and architecture that ages gracefully.
